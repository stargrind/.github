## Prior experience?
No.\*

*\*Well, sort of.*

There's rudimentary experience in most fields, but by and large this is a thing of simply extreme interest rather than pre-existing knowledge.

I (again, `shinatama`) very easily get caught up in "what's the best way to do this?" and that is often beneficial, but thanks to psenough's lovely [guidebook](https://github.com/psenough/teach_yourself_demoscene_in_14_days), I have adopted the philosophy of simply trying to do [Programming, Motherfucker](https://programming-motherfucker.com/) and while the code I write may not be particularly impressive or well-made, we are all learning, and will hone ourselves in time. At the end of the day, it's computer art, right? Like all art, not every piece is going to be the Mona Lisa initially, and I have beyond accepted that. The hope is to try our best, have fun, learn, and keep moving on the road ahead! ^_^
